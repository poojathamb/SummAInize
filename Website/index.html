SummAInize

Hit your free limit? Open your old chat, click once, and get a short summary any AI can read. Paste it into a new account or a different tool and keep going.

Download the extension

Free. Works with Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Gemini and more.

How it works

  1. Open your old chatIt still opens and reads fine, even when you can’t send new messages.
  2. Click SummAInizeIt reads the conversation and writes a short hand-off summary: your goal, decisions, current state and next steps.
  3. Copy and pastePress Copy, open a new chat on any AI tool, and paste. No need to explain everything again.

Install in two minutes

Chrome, Edge or Brave

  1. Download and unzip the file.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (edge://extensions in Edge).
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.

Safari on Mac

  1. Download and unzip the file.
  2. In Safari, open Settings, then Developer, and tick Allow unsigned extensions.
  3. Click Add Temporary Extension and choose the folder.
  4. In Settings, then Extensions, turn it on and set website access to Allow.

For the AI summary, add your own free Gemini API key or any other API key in the extension’s Settings. Without a key, SummAInize still works in Trim only mode.
